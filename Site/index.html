Guides & Help

Learn how to use the features in ZAS Connect.

Updated Patient Directory

The Patient Directory has been revamped to allow faster searching by Name, ID, or Date of Birth. You can now quickly view the ward and status of each patient directly from the main list. Click 'View' to access full patient details.

  1. Navigate to the Patient Directory from the sidebar.
  2. Click on the Search Patient input field.
  3. Type in the patient's Name, ID, or Date of Birth.
  4. Click the Search button.
  5. Click View on the desired patient row to see full details.

Enhanced Patient Records

Adding and updating clinical notes is now more intuitive. Ensure you have the patient ID ready. You can specify the type of record (Lab Results, X-Ray, etc.) and add detailed text notes. The unified timeline shows all updates from the care team.

  1. Go to the Patient Records section.
  2. Click the + Update Record button at the top right.
  3. Fill in the Patient ID (e.g., PT-1029).
  4. Select the Date, Hour, and Type.
  5. Add detailed clinical information in the Notes field.
  6. Click Save Record to update the patient's timeline.

Secure Communications

Exchange data securely with other medical staff without leaving the portal. Use the 'New Message' button to draft communications to specific doctors or departments. All messages are encrypted and logged for compliance.

  1. Open the Secure Communications tab.
  2. Click the New Message button at the bottom.
  3. In the modal, enter the recipient's name or department in the To field.
  4. Provide a Subject and write your Message.
  5. Click Send. The message will appear in your timeline.
