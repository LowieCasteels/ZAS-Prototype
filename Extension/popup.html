Guides & Help

Patient Directory

Search and manage patient profiles faster than ever.

  1. Navigate to the Patient Directory from the sidebar.
  2. Click on the Search Patient input field.
  3. Type in the patient's Name, ID, or Date of Birth and click Search.

Patient Records

Adding and updating clinical notes is now more intuitive.

  1. Go to the Patient Records section.
  2. Click the + Update Record button.
  3. Fill in the Patient ID, Date, and Type.
  4. Click Save Record to update the patient's timeline.
